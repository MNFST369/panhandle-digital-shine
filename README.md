# MARS CONSULTING

Build a sleek, modern, single-page landing website for "Mars Consulting," a web design and digital marketing consulting business based in Panama City Beach, FL, serving local businesses and individuals across the Florida Panhandle.

BUSINESS CONTEXT

Mars Consulting builds websites, landing pages, Google Business listings, and social media profiles for small businesses and individuals who want a stronger online presence but find technology intimidating or overwhelming. Every part of the site should feel simple, clear, and reassuring — no jargon, no clutter, big readable text, obvious next steps.

VISUAL STYLE

Coastal and professional. Primary palette: deep ocean blue and crisp white, with a warm sand/tan neutral for secondary sections, and one warm accent color (coral or gold) reserved for buttons and calls to action. Clean sans-serif typography, generous white space, subtle rounded corners, soft shadows. Modern but approachable — not corporate-cold, not overly playful.

STRUCTURE

One-page scrolling layout with a sticky top navigation bar (logo left, anchor links right: Home, Services, Work, About, Contact). Smooth-scroll to sections. Fully responsive and mobile-first, since many visitors will browse on their phones. Large tap targets and legible text sizes throughout — this audience is not tech-savvy, so avoid tiny buttons, dense text blocks, or unexplained icons.

SECTIONS (in order):

1. HERO

Full-width hero with the headline "More Customers Start Online. Let's Get You Found." and subheadline "Websites, Google listings, and social profiles built for Panama City Beach and Panhandle businesses — no tech headaches, just results." Primary CTA button: "Get Your Free Consultation," scrolling to the contact form. Background: a soft coastal gradient or subtle wave/beach-inspired graphic — tasteful, not busy.

2. SERVICES

Four-card grid, each with an icon, short title, and one-sentence description:

- Website Design — "A professional site that makes a great first impression and turns visitors into customers."

- Landing Pages — "A focused, high-converting page for a specific promotion, product, or campaign."

- Google Business Listings — "Show up when people search for you, with accurate hours, photos, and reviews that build trust."

- Social Media Profiles — "Professional, on-brand social pages so you look active and credible online."

3. WHY MARS CONSULTING

A short trust-building section aimed at reassuring non-technical visitors. Three or four short points with icons, such as: "No tech jargon — we explain everything in plain English," "We handle setup from start to finish," "Local to Panama City Beach," "Personal support, not a call center."

4. FEATURED WORK

A responsive grid or carousel of 4–6 portfolio cards (use placeholder images and labels for now — real project screenshots and names will be swapped in later). Each card: project image, project name, one-line description, and a category tag (e.g. "Website," "Landing Page," "Social Media").

5. CONTACT

A clean, simple contact form as the main conversion point, with fields for:

- Name

- Phone number

- Email

- What service are you interested in? (dropdown: Website Design / Landing Page / Google Business Listing / Social Media Profiles / Not Sure — Help Me Decide)

- Optional short message field: "Tell us a bit about your business" (easy to remove if you want to keep it to exactly 4 fields)

Include a reassuring line above the form, like "Fill this out and we'll reach out within one business day — no pressure, no obligation." Show a phone number and email as an alternate contact method next to the form.

6. FOOTER

Business name (Mars Consulting), short tagline, service area ("Proudly serving Panama City Beach and the Florida Panhandle"), contact info, placeholder social icons, and copyright line.

GENERAL NOTES

Keep the whole experience simple and confidence-inspiring — this is for people who are intimidated by technology, not tech enthusiasts. Prioritize clarity over cleverness: obvious navigation, short sentences, clear buttons, no unexplained jargon. Do not show pricing anywhere — services are quote-based, so every CTA should point toward the contact form. Fast loading, clean code, accessible color contrast. Meta title and description should mention Panama City Beach and the Florida Panhandle for local search.
